What we help your team do

Most firms already have AI tools within reach. What is missing is a shared, safe way to use them. That is the part we teach.

Team training

Hands-on sessions built around your own documents, emails and client work, so people leave able to use AI the next morning.

Safe-use guidelines

Plain rules for what can go into an AI tool and what never should, written for your firm and the confidentiality you owe your clients.

Ongoing support

A monthly arrangement that keeps your team current as the tools change, with someone to ask when a new question comes up.

A real person teaches your team.

Every session is led live by a person who learns how your firm works, answers questions as they come up, and works through real tasks alongside your people. You are not handed a video library and left to figure it out.

AI compliance

Financial and professional firms answer to clients, regulators and their own policies. We help you set and write down how AI may be used, which client information stays out of it, and who checks the output, so your practice holds up when someone asks. Your attorney or compliance officer keeps the final say.

Efficiency on day one

The first session is built on tasks your team already does every week, so the time savings start that day instead of after a long rollout.

How it works

Three steps, sized to a small or mid-sized firm.

  1. We talk

    A short conversation about how your team works today, which tools you already pay for, and where time is going.

  2. We train

    Sessions on site or by video, using real tasks from your office. Your team practices, and we write down what worked.

  3. We stay available

    Follow-up each month so new habits stick and new tools get a fair, careful look before anyone relies on them.

Where we work. In person around Burlington, Plattsburgh, Saratoga Springs and across Vermont and the Adirondacks. By video anywhere.
